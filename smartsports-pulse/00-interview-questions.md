# Prompt 0 — Kickoff interview

Blog: SmartSports Pulse  
Candidate domain: smart-sportshq.com  
Working language: English  
Site language: Korean (English only for terms/brands with no natural Korean equivalent)

This file is questions only. No categories, keywords, sitemap, or strategy.

---

## Audience

1. When a first-time reader and a returning reader want different things, who do we write for first? Say which person, in one sentence — not “both.”
2. What does that person already know on day one: sports rules only, they have already placed a wager, they already read Korean sports media, or something else you will name?
3. After they finish a typical post, what should they be able to *do* that they could not do before? Name the action. “Understand better” is too vague.
4. “Beginner sports fans and bettors” is two jobs. If a post that helps a fan watch a match well would *not* help someone read a price, which job wins?

## Purpose

5. You listed five 90-day outcomes: approachable educational hub, complete beginner-guide library, clear odds-breakdown resources, steady Google indexing, fully functional site layout. Which single miss means the 90 days failed?
6. Is the site’s job to help someone decide whether a price is worth taking, or to help them watch/analyze a match without taking a price? If you want both, which one is the homepage promise?

## Voice

7. In one phrase you will stand behind, how should the Korean prose feel: classroom teacher, older sibling explaining a match, news desk, or another voice you will name?
8. How much English is acceptable inside a Korean paragraph — brand names only, plus common market terms (e.g. handicap, odds), plus stats jargon (e.g. xG), or something stricter you will specify?
9. What tone is banned even if it gets clicks — guaranteed-sounding language is already out; do you also ban picks, slang, talking down to beginners, hype, or something else you will name?

## Topics

10. Which sports are in scope for the first published version? List them. I will not add a league or sport you do not list.
11. Of the three jobs you already named — betting-strategy how-to, sports-analysis rules, odds breakdowns — which one is the core of the first published set? If you say all three, which one gets the first ten posts?
12. Which reader requests will you refuse even if they ask — match picks, live in-play how-to, player props, a specific league, a specific operator, or something else you will name?
13. Should early posts be evergreen explainers only, or also time-bound match write-ups? If both, which one ships first?

## Constraints

14. The title ban lists English words (`bet`, `casino`, `slots`, `gambling`). Are Korean terms such as 베팅, 토토, 프로토, 픽 also banned? Say where: page titles, H1, body, meta description, or all of them.
15. You did not approve affiliate banners. An unpublished site branch still ships banner slots. For a Korean audience, private books are not a legal destination. Are banners in v1 at all? If yes, what is the only destination you will allow — a partner URL you will paste, official legal channel only, or internal pages until you name a partner?
16. Is `smart-sportshq.com` purchased and under your DNS control, or still only a candidate? If Pulse and HQ disagree as brand signals, which string is the public name on the homepage?
17. Besides the title ban and no guaranteed-profit / no illegal-site rules: 19+ on every page? Any other legal or brand line I must not cross?
18. There is already an unpublished site branch with invented categories, a tagline, and dozens of posts you did not approve in this interview. For this foundation, do I ignore that branch until you approve structure, or audit it against your answers?

## Ops reality

19. Who actually writes the Korean — you, an AI draft you will edit, a native Korean editor, or a mix you will describe? Who is the last human to approve a post before it goes live? What publish cadence can that person keep for 90 days without burnout — two posts a month, weekly, or another number you will own? (Daily with no named writer is not a plan.)
20. Who owns domain DNS, hosting, Google Search Console, and Naver Search Advisor — you, someone you will name, or “not set up yet”?
