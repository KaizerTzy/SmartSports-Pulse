## Rules (do not stuff)

- Titles, H1, slug, meta follow the brand-safety ban. **Do not target** as chrome keywords: bet, casino, slots, gambling, 카지노, 슬롯, 사설토토, 바카라, 꽁머니, 먹튀, or 베팅/토토 in those fields.  
- Body may use 베팅/토토 when explaining legal 프로토·스포츠토토 **formats**. That is not a license to rank for 토토 사이트.  
- **오버언더 vs 언더오버 (locked):** Titles, H1, slug, meta stay **오버언더**. In article body, use Betman’s official term **언더오버** (`betman.co.kr`) as a natural secondary synonym. Name it as terminology, not as a shop: no purchase CTA, no review, no “where to play.”  
- One primary idea per URL. Long-tails support the outline; they are not extra titles.  
- Intent we want: **informational**. Commercial “where to play / 추천 사이트” and pick-intent “오늘 분석” fight positioning — **rejected**, even if louder in SERPs.  
- Brand keywords are for **own-brand clarity** (people who already heard the name), not a vanity traffic plan.

**Walk-away (high estimated demand, wrong job):** 토토 사이트, 단폴 사이트, 사설토토, 프로토 픽, 오늘 승부 예측, 베트맨 구매, 적중률, 보장 수익.

## Title pattern ideas (voice-safe)

Match 존댓말, hype 1, locked titles 1–10. Formulas — not extra invented titles.

1. `{용어}, {금액}이면 얼마가 되나`  
2. `{용어} 읽는 법 ({예시})`  
3. `{용어}가 의미하는 것 (가상 {종목} 예시)`  
4. `{A}와 {B}의 차이, {구조적 결과}`  
5. `{숫자}, {오해}는 다르다`

**Do not use:** 완벽 정리, 꿀팁, 필수, 지금, 100%, 추천, 적중, 2026년 최신, 사이트 모음.

Working titles 1–10 already follow 1–5. Keep them; do not rewrite for stuffing.

## Meta description rules

| | Rule |
|---|---|
| Length | **70–100자** (spaces included). **Estimate:** Korean SERP truncation often ~70–80자; stay in band, do not pad. |
| Voice | 존댓말, Calm Analyst. Outcome the reader can **do**. |
| Content | Name the mechanic + 가상 예시 or ₩ amount when true. |
| Must not | Banned chrome words; 가입/추천 사이트; 예측·픽·보장; keyword lists; “SS Pulse” instead of the page topic. |
| CTA | None. No “지금 확인하세요.” |
| Brand | Optional ` \| SmartSports Pulse` only if the sentence is still ≤100자. Skip if it crowds the outcome. |

**Pattern (not for stuffing):** `{이 글을 읽으면 ~할 수 있습니다}. 가상 예시로 {숫자}를 계산합니다.`
