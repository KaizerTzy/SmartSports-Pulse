## Audience

### 1. Who is the real reader?

An enthusiast sports fan who would like to try sports betting sites. The real priority is the complete beginner who is interested to learn safely.

### 2. What are they trying to do in that moment?

Come to browse basic educational content: understanding how sports odds calculations work, learning the core rules for sports betting options, and seeing step-by-step examples of how a typical wager functions.

### 3. Which sports stay?

- Primary focus (would never cut): European football, and baseball (KBO & MLB).
- Secondary focus: basketball (NBA & KBL), and esports (League of Legends).
- If forced to launch extremely narrow, European football and KBO/MLB baseball are the top two that stay.

### 4. Who is not the reader?

- People hunting for predictions, direct match-winning picks, or tipster claims
- Professional betting players
- Minors (under 19)

Red-flag rule: “This article is for the wrong person” if it promises guaranteed profits, focuses on casino/non-sports content, or uses hype-heavy “VIP pick” language instead of educational math/analysis explanations.

## Purpose

### 5. What does 90-day success feel like?

Success means clicking around the site and feeling like I’m looking at a clean, professional, and accessible resource hub — something I could confidently share with a friend who wants to learn sports analysis without getting overwhelmed. Winning means having clear, easy-to-read guides on odds calculation and match rules live, accompanied by a polished site layout.

### 6. After ~10 minutes, what can a first-time visitor do?

- Read an odds board (e.g. Decimal, or Korean Proto formats) and calculate their exact potential payout on a KRW 10,000 or 100,000 wager.
- Explain the core difference between basic betting markets like Money line, Point Spread/Handicap, and Over/Under.

### 7. What is this site for?

This site is designed as an educational directory and sports analysis portal. Similar in concept to popular Korean portal sites like Totohot.net, but focused strictly on beginner education, odds analysis guides, and sports rules. In the long run, it will serve as a trusted Korean portal where users can learn sports analysis fundamentals and view compliant ad banner placements.

## Voice

### 8. Tone wanted / refused

- Desired tone: “Calm Analyst” or “Supportive Guide” — informative, clear, approachable, and encouraging without being overwhelming.
- Refused tone: Strictly no hype touts, slang-heavy tipster energy (“VIP picks”, “100% win rate”), or stiff, boring textbook academic language.

### 9. Korean register, slang, English mix

- Formality: Polite 존댓말 (e.g. ~합니다 / ~해요) to keep it respectful and professional for a general audience.
- Slang level: Minimal to standard. Common sports / sports-toto terms understood by Korean fans are fine in body copy (e.g. 승무패, 오버언더, 핸디캡, 배당), but avoid illegal gambling forum jargon.
- Language mix: Keep primary text in natural Korean. Retain widely used English terms in English script or standard Korean transliteration where natural (e.g. KBL, MLB, NBA, Odds, Handicap, Over/Under).

### 10. Brand name display

- Intentional mix: Yes, using an English brand name and domain alongside Korean content is intentional.
- Display rule: Use both. Display as “SmartSports Pulse (under: 스마트스포츠 펄스)” in headers/about sections, and “SmartSports Pulse” for official branding, footers, and domain contexts.

## Topics

### 11. First questions beginners actually ask

- How do I calculate and how much money I will actually win from these odds?
- What does Handicap (-1.5) or Over/Under (2.5) mean?
- What is the difference between single bets, multi bets, and parlay bets?
- What are the rules on baseball betting?

### 12. Center of gravity

- Primary focus: Odds explanation & calculation.
- Supporting focus: Sports betting rules & market explanations (Handicap, Over/Under).
- Drop first if time is short: Advanced betting strategy. (Fundamentals and odds math come before strategy.)

### 13. Predictions / picks in scope?

Match predictions, “who will win” picks, or tipster content are not in scope as standalone posts.

Example matches may only be used as hypothetical, generic educational illustrations inside explainers (e.g. using a mock KBO match to show how Over/Under payout math works). No live pick advice.

### 14. Off-limits in the content itself

- Off-limits topics: Online casino games (baccarat, blackjack, slots), live minigames, non-sports novelty bets, and crypto/bitcoin gambling.
- Off-limits angles: Promising “guaranteed profits,” bankroll-doubling tricks, martingale systems, or reviewing/linking to unregulated/illegal private betting sites.

## Constraints

### 15. How far the title ban extends

- Extent of title ban: The strict ban on explicit gambling promotional terms applies to page titles, H1 headings, URLs (slugs), and meta descriptions.
- Body copy / explanations: Educational terms like “베팅” or “토토” are allowed inside body copy only when explaining rules, math, or legal Sports Toto formats.
- Banned Korean title words: Avoid explicit commercial gambling words in titles like 카지노 (casino), 슬롯 (slots), 사설토토 (illegal toto), 바카라 (baccarat), 꽁머니 (free money/bonuses), and 먹튀 (scam/withdrawal issues). Use safe educational terms instead (배당, 분석, 가이드, 핸디캡, 승무패, 프로토).

### 16. Publisher / legal notices

- Purpose: Strictly 100% educational and sports analysis content only. The site does not accept, process, or facilitate bets.
- Required notices: Every page must feature a footer disclaimer stating:
  - a) “본 사이트는 스포츠 분석 및 교육용 정보를 제공하며, 어떠한 형태의 실제 베팅도 진행하지 않습니다.” (This site provides sports analysis and educational information only and does not process any form of actual betting.)
  - b) Standard 19+ age restriction notice and responsible analysis warning.

(Country of operation was not specified in the answer.)

### 17. Platforms, ads, screenshots, reviews

- Platform promotion & content: Article content remains strictly neutral, educational, and objective — no hard-selling, pushy recommendations, or “where to bet” calls to action.
- Display ad banners: Allowed and expected. Static affiliate/partner display banners will sit in standard sidebar or footer ad blocks to capture passive traffic without forcing clicks on the reader.
- Screenshots & visuals: Allowed using custom-edited diagrams, charts, and odds mockups (designed in any editor apps) to keep all images 100% original, copyright-free, and easy for beginners to read.
- Reviews & comparisons: Forbidden for v1. The focus is purely on teaching odds math and analysis rules rather than ranking betting platforms.

### 18. Kill a draft on sight

- Any article that claims a “guaranteed win” system, “100% profit strategy,” or “VIP pick service.”
- Any content mentioning casino games, minigames, or non-sports gambling topics.
- Low-quality, thin AI-generated articles filled with repetitive fluff instead of real, practical math breakdowns and clear visual examples.

## Ops reality

### 19. Who writes, and real throughput

- Writing/editing process: AI-assisted drafts generated, which I personally review, edit, and publish.
- Sustainable real throughput: 2 to 3 high-quality educational articles per week (approx. 8 or 9–12 comprehensive guides per month). This guarantees well-structured, thoroughly reviewed content instead of thin, rushed posts.

### 20. What exists vs undecided; launch cadence

- What already exists: A clear content framework for beginner odds math (Decimal/American/Proto formats), basic sports market rules (Handicap/Over-Under), and custom graphic templates via any editor tool.
- Undecided / flexible: Final automated deployment pipeline and exact indexing timeline for search engines.
- Launch cadence: Batch launch first (a core set of 5–8 foundational guides to give the site an immediate educational base), followed by a steady weekly posting schedule of 2–3 posts per week.
