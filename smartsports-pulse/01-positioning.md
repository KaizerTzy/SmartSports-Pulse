## Content mission (one sentence)

SmartSports Pulse teaches complete-beginner Korean sports fans to read a Decimal or Korean Proto odds board, calculate an exact payout on a ₩10,000 or ₩100,000 example stake, and explain Money line / 승무패, Handicap, and Over/Under — using hypothetical match math, not picks.

That is the reader outcome. “Quality hub,” “sports analysis portal,” and “beginner education” are not the mission unless they produce that skill.

---

## Primary audience

**Who:** A Korean sports enthusiast who is a complete beginner to sports wagering and wants to learn how odds and basic markets work *before* (or instead of) using a betting site. Not a working bettor looking for an edge.

**Language / region:** Korean copy, South Korean market; publisher is remote/global. Examples use KRW. Common fan terms (승무패, 오버언더, 핸디캡, 배당, 프로토) and widely used English labels (MLB, KBO, NBA, KBL, Odds, Handicap, Over/Under) stay as specified.

**Expertise level:** Zero-to-first-lesson. They can follow European football and/or baseball (KBO, MLB) as fans. They cannot yet turn an odds number into a payout or tell Handicap (−1.5) from Over/Under (2.5).

**Sports gravity (not a category map):** Launch meaning is tied to European football and baseball (KBO & MLB). Basketball (NBA & KBL) and esports (League of Legends) are secondary and are not required to prove the mission.

### Who we are not for

- Anyone hunting predictions, “who wins,” or tipster/VIP pick claims  
- Professional or high-volume betting players  
- Minors (under 19)  
- Readers looking for casino, slots, minigames, novelty bets, crypto gambling, 사설토토, 꽁머니, or 먹튀 content  
- Readers who want platform rankings or “where to bet”

**Wrong-person test:** If a page promises guaranteed profit, leaves sports for casino, or sounds like a VIP pick — it is for the wrong person.

## Differentiator

A generic niche betting blog teaches “what is a handicap” in English, or publishes picks. A typical Korean toto-style portal (you named Totohot.net as a *concept* reference only) is built around match boards, traffic, and site-directory energy.

Here the reader gets something those two usually do not combine:

1. **Worked KRW payout math** on Decimal and Korean Proto boards (₩10,000 / ₩100,000), with original diagrams — not a definition dump.  
2. **Market literacy** (Money line / 승무패, Handicap, Over/Under, and how a single wager *functions*) in polite 존댓말, using mock matches only.  
3. **No pick, no platform sell, no 사설.** Neutral explainers. The site does not take bets. “분석” here means reading numbers and rules, not forecasting a winner.

If a draft cannot help the reader compute a payout or state the difference between those markets, it is not differentiated. Sidebar/footer ad banners are a distribution choice, not the differentiator.

**Challenge:** Calling this a “portal like Totohot.net” is a layout/ambition metaphor. Totohot.net is not a beginner classroom, and a first batch of 5–8 guides is a **library**, not a live portal. Phase 1 should sound like a calm, shareable classroom with a clean hub layout — not like a toto front page. Approve or reject that framing below.
