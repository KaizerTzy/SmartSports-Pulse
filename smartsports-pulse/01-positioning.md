## Content mission (one sentence)

SmartSports Pulse teaches complete-beginner Korean sports fans to read a Decimal or Korean Proto odds board, calculate an exact ₩10,000 or ₩100,000 payout, and see how a single wager differs from a multi/parlay (including how odds multiply and risk grows with each added selection) — using hypothetical match math, not picks.

Handicap, Over/Under, 승무패, and Money line are Phase 1 lessons that follow that core skill. They are not the first thing the reader must be able to do. American odds are not part of this mission until after the launch set.

“Quality hub,” “sports analysis portal,” and “beginner education” are not the mission unless they produce that skill.

---

## Primary audience

**Who:** A Korean sports enthusiast who is a complete beginner to sports wagering and wants to learn how odds and basic markets work *before* (or instead of) using a betting site. Not a working bettor looking for an edge.

**Language / region:** Korean copy, South Korean market; publisher is remote/global. Examples use KRW. Common fan terms (승무패, 오버언더, 핸디캡, 배당, 프로토) and widely used English labels (MLB, KBO, NBA, KBL, Odds, Handicap, Over/Under) stay as specified.

**Expertise level:** Zero-to-first-lesson. They can follow European football and/or baseball (KBO, MLB) as fans. They cannot yet turn an odds number into a payout, tell a single wager from a parlay, or tell Handicap (−1.5) from Over/Under (2.5).

**Sports gravity (not a category map):** Launch meaning is tied to European football and baseball (KBO & MLB). Basketball (NBA & KBL) and esports (League of Legends) are secondary and are not required to prove the mission.

### Who we are not for

- Anyone hunting predictions, “who wins,” or tipster/VIP pick claims  
- Professional or high-volume betting players  
- Minors (under 19)  
- Readers looking for casino, slots, minigames, novelty bets, crypto gambling, 사설토토, 꽁머니, or 먹튀 content  
- Readers who want platform rankings or “where to bet”

**Wrong-person test:** If a page promises guaranteed profit, leaves sports for casino, or sounds like a VIP pick — it is for the wrong person.

## Differentiator

A generic niche betting blog teaches “what is a handicap” in English, or publishes picks. A typical Korean toto-style portal (you named Totohot.net as a *concept* reference only) is built around match boards, traffic, and site-directory energy.

Here the reader gets something those two usually do not combine:

1. **Worked KRW payout math** on Decimal and Korean Proto boards (₩10,000 / ₩100,000), with original diagrams — not a definition dump.  
2. **Wager-structure literacy first:** how a single bet pays versus how parlay/multi odds multiply and how risk scales when selections are added — before market types.  
3. **Then market literacy as separate lessons:** 승무패, Money line, Handicap, Over/Under — polite 존댓말, mock matches only.  
4. **No pick, no platform sell, no 사설.** Neutral explainers. The site does not take bets.

**Locked meaning of “분석”:** Educational market mechanics and odds math using hypothetical examples only. Never live prediction advice, never “who wins,” never an implied cover/lean — even inside a hypothetical.

If a draft cannot help the reader compute a payout or see how a parlay’s odds and risk change when a selection is added, it is not differentiated. Sidebar/footer ad banners are a distribution choice, not the differentiator.

**Phase 1 shape (locked):** A **static educational hub** — a small library of guides plus a clean, shareable layout. Not a live toto front page. Totohot.net remains inspiration only for “I could send this to a friend,” not for match boards or site-directory jobs.

## Out of scope for Phase 1

Deliberately skipped at launch. Not a “misc” pile — these are no’s.

**Topics**

- Standalone match predictions, live picks, tipster/VIP content  
- Advanced betting strategy (including martingale, bankroll-doubling, “guaranteed” systems)  
- Casino, baccarat, blackjack, slots, live minigames, non-sports novelty bets, crypto/bitcoin gambling  
- 사설토토 / unregulated private sites; 먹튀 and 꽁머니 angles  
- Betting-platform reviews, rankings, or comparisons (forbidden for v1)  
- American odds (deferred until after the first launch batch)  
- Secondary sports as a launch requirement: NBA, KBL, League of Legends (they may appear later; they do not define Phase 1)

**Formats**

- Thin, repetitive AI fluff without practical math and a clear visual example  
- Live “who wins tonight” posts; real fixtures used as advice rather than generic hypotheticals  
- Copyrighted/unedited third-party odds screenshots (visuals are original mockups/diagrams)  
- Live updating odds/match boards or automated match listings  
- Combining 승무패 and Money line into one lesson (they are two lessons)

**CTAs / site behaviors**

- “Where to bet,” hard-sell recommendations, or in-article pushes to a bookmaker  
- Accepting, processing, or facilitating bets  
- Title / H1 / slug / meta use of promotional gambling terms (including 카지노, 슬롯, 사설토토, 바카라, 꽁머니, 먹튀)  
- Any page without the educational-only footer disclaimer and 19+ / responsible-analysis notice

**Phase 1 does include** (order is editorial, not a category map):

1. Decimal + Korean Proto odds math, with ₩10,000 / ₩100,000 payout examples  
2. Single vs multi/parlay at the **front** of the launch set, alongside that odds math  
3. Then two **separate** lessons: 승무패, and Money line  
4. Then Handicap and Over/Under  
5. A polished static hub layout; static affiliate/partner banners in sidebar or footer (passive, not in-copy CTAs)

## Clarifications — answered and applied

1. **Phase 1 format:** Static educational hub. No live updating boards or automated match listings.  
2. **“분석”:** Strictly educational market mechanics and odds math using hypothetical examples. Never live prediction advice or “who wins” picks.  
3. **American odds:** After the first batch. Launch set = Decimal + Korean Proto only.  
4. **승무패 vs Money line:** Two separate lessons.  
5. **Single vs multi/parlay:** Front of the launch set, alongside odds math, **before** Handicap or Over/Under. Beginners must see how a single bet works versus how parlay odds multiply and how risk scales with added selections.
