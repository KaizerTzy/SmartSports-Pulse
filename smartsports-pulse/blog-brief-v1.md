## 3. Positioning

**Mission:** SmartSports Pulse teaches complete-beginner Korean sports fans to read a Decimal or Korean Proto odds board, calculate an exact ₩10,000 or ₩100,000 payout, and see how a single wager differs from a multi/parlay (odds multiply; risk grows with each added selection) — using hypothetical match math, not picks.

Handicap, Over/Under, 승무패, and Money line follow that core skill. American odds are not in the launch mission.

**“분석” means:** educational market mechanics and odds math with hypothetical examples. Never a live prediction, never “who wins,” never an implied cover/lean.

**Reader:** A Korean sports enthusiast, complete beginner to wagering, who wants to learn odds and basic markets before (or instead of) using a betting site. Follows European football and/or baseball (KBO, MLB) as a fan. Cannot yet turn a number into a payout or tell Handicap (−1.5) from Over/Under (2.5).

**Not for:** pick/VIP hunters; professional or high-volume players; minors (under 19); casino / 사설 / 꽁머니 / 먹튀 readers; platform-ranking shoppers.

**Wrong-person test:** If a page promises guaranteed profit, leaves sports for casino, or sounds like a VIP pick, it is for the wrong person.

**Differentiator:** Worked KRW math on Decimal and 프로토 boards; combination literacy (단폴 vs 다폴) before market types; then separate market lessons; no picks, no platform sell, no 사설. Original diagrams. Neutral body.

**Phase 1 shape:** Static educational hub. Library of guides + clean layout. Not a live toto front page, not match listings.

**Out of scope (Phase 1):** predictions/picks; advanced strategy (martingale, bankroll-doubling, guaranteed systems); casino, minigames, novelty, crypto gambling; 사설토토 / 먹튀 / 꽁머니; platform reviews or “where to bet”; American odds in the launch set; NBA/KBL/LoL as launch requirements; K리그; live boards; thin AI fluff; third-party odds screenshots; combining 승무패 and Money line into one post; in-article bookmaker CTAs; taking or facilitating bets.

## 5. Voice + brand safety

**Tone (1–5):** Formality 4 · Humor 1–2 · Hype 1 · Technical depth 3 · Address: supportive 2nd person, 존댓말.

### Footer (every page)

1. Brand: SmartSports Pulse  
2. `본 사이트는 스포츠 분석 및 교육용 정보를 제공하며, 어떠한 형태의 실제 베팅도 진행하지 않습니다.`

5. Links: 소개 (`/about/`) · 개인정보처리방침 (`/privacy/`)

### DO

- Korean 존댓말; allowed English labels as listed in §1.  
- ₩10,000 / ₩100,000 worked examples; Decimal and 프로토 in the launch set.  
- Hypothetical matches labeled 가상. Teams: **팀 A / 팀 B**, **홈 / 원정** only. No invented club names. No real player names in Phase 1 math.  
- Original mockups only.  
- 베팅 / 토토 in **body** only when explaining rules, math, or legal Sports Toto / 프로토 formats.  
- Neutral body. Byline SS Pulse Team.  
- Titles/H1/slug/meta: prefer 배당, 분석, 가이드, 핸디캡, 승무패, 프로토 where they fit. 분석 = mechanics, never a lean.  
- Titles stay **오버언더**. Body may use Betman (`betman.co.kr`) **언더오버** as a terminology synonym once — not a shop CTA.

### DON’T

- Guaranteed win/profit, 100% 적중, 보장 수익, VIP 픽, martingale, implied winner/cover/lean.  
- Casino, 바카라, 슬롯, crypto gambling, novelty, 사설토토, 먹튀, 꽁머니, 사이트 추천, 총판.  
- Platform reviews; “where to bet”; live boards; AI 예측.  
- Minors as audience.  
- Title/H1/slug/meta: bet, casino, slots, gambling; 카지노, 슬롯, 사설토토, 바카라, 꽁머니, 먹튀; 베팅/토토 in those chrome fields.  
- In-article affiliate links or bookmaker CTAs. Ad chrome **empty at launch**.  
- Third-party screenshots; real upcoming fixtures as advice; naming real bettors.
