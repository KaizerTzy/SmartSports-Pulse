## Tone dials

Scale: 1 = low, 5 = high. Phase 1 sits in the middle-right of *clarity*, not of *energy*.

| Dial | Score | How it sounds |
|---|---|---|
| Formality | **4** | Polite 존댓말 (`~합니다` for explanations, `~해요` when inviting the reader to try a step). Not 반말. Not 5: no stiff textbook, no jargon wall. |
| Humor | **1–2** | Warm enough to stay human. No jokes, memes, or “형/누나” banter. No tipster slang as punchline. |
| Urgency / hype | **1** | Never FOMO, never “지금,” never VIP, never 100%. The page can wait; the math cannot be faked. |
| Technical depth | **3** | Worked KRW arithmetic and settlement rules. Not academic probability theory. Not a definition with no numbers. |
| How we address the reader | **Supportive 2nd person, 존댓말** | 독자에게 계산을 보여 준다. Not “우리 픽.” Not lecturing “당신은 초보라서.” Encourage without cheering a wager. |

**Persona in prose:** Calm Analyst + Supportive Guide (your labels). One voice, even when drafts start as AI: you rewrite until it sounds like a patient explainer, not a generator.

## Hard DO / DON’T (brand safety) — approved

If a draft violates DON’T, kill it even if the SEO looks good.

### DO

- Write Korean 존댓말; keep English labels you already allowed (MLB, KBO, Odds, Handicap, Over/Under, Money line).  
- Use ₩10,000 / ₩100,000 worked examples on Decimal and 프로토 boards.  
- Use **hypothetical** matches only; label them 가상. Teams: **팀 A / 팀 B** and **홈 / 원정** only. Do not invent club names.  
- Use original diagrams/mockups (copyright-free).  
- Allow 베팅 / 토토 in **body** only when explaining rules, math, or legal Sports Toto / 프로토 formats.  
- Keep article body **neutral** — teach the mechanic, do not steer a wager.  
- Include the educational footer + 19+ placeholder + footer-only non-prediction line on every page.  
- Address complete beginners; say who the page is not for when needed (not a pick).  
- Prefer 단폴/다폴, 승무패, 오버언더, 핸디캡, 배당 in body — standard fan terms, not 사설 forum slang.  
- Byline: SS Pulse Team.

### DON’T

**Claims we never make**

- Guaranteed win/profit, 100% 적중, 보장 수익, 원금 두 배, “실패 없는,” VIP 픽, 전문가 확정.  
- Martingale / 마틴게일 / any bankroll-doubling “system.”  
- Implied winner, cover, or over/under **lean** — even in a hypothetical (“이 스코어면 이 팀이 유리”). Settlement math only.  
- “분석” as a match prediction.  
- We take bets, process bets, or “help you place” a wager.

**Topics we never cover (Phase 1 and kill-on-sight)**

- Casino, 바카라, 블랙잭, 슬롯, 룰렛, live minigames, game-show tables.  
- Crypto / bitcoin / USDT gambling.  
- Non-sports novelty markets.  
- 사설토토, 먹튀, 꽁머니, 메이저 사이트 추천, 총판.  
- Platform reviews, rankings, “where to bet,” overseas-site shopping.  
- Live boards, tonight’s card, AI 예측, confidence %.  
- Minors (under 19) as an audience; no “청소년도.”  
- American odds in the launch set (deferred, not a never-forever topic).  
- NBA/KBL/롤 as launch topics; K리그 (not approved).  
- Thin AI fluff with no math and no diagram.

**Titles / H1 / slug / meta — never**

- English: bet, casino, slots, gambling (and obvious variants).  
- Korean: 카지노, 슬롯, 사설토토, 바카라, 꽁머니, 먹튀.  
- 베팅 / 토토 in those chrome fields (body only, as above).

**Privacy / third parties**

- Do not name real bettors, readers, or “제보된 피해자.”  
- Do not use a real upcoming fixture as advice.  
- Do not invent or libel operators (“○○ 먹튀”).  
- Do not copy third-party odds screenshots or player photos.  
- League names (KBO, MLB, European football) as **labels** are fine. No real player names in Phase 1 math. Teams: 팀 A / 팀 B, 홈 / 원정.  
- Do not republish commenters’ real names or account IDs.

**Promo / legal**

- No in-article affiliate or bookmaker CTA.  
- No illegal-site links.  
- No ad banners at launch (empty chrome). When banners exist later, no “가입하면 된다” adjacent to them.  
- Publisher does not accept or facilitate bets (remote/global, Korean market).
